# Contract Review Framework

## Pre-Review
- Confirm business objective and responsible owner.

## Analysis
- Verify liability limitations.
- Check termination clauses and renewal terms.
- Assess IP ownership and confidentiality.
- Evaluate payment and delivery obligations.

## Output
- **Risk Level:** [Low/Med/High]
- **Impact:** [Financial/Operational/Legal]
- **Required Actions:** [Redlines/Negotiation]
- **Approval Guidance:** [Approve / Reject / Conditional]
