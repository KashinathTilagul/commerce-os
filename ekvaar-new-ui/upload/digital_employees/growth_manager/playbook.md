# Growth Playbook

## Growth Methodology
For every opportunity:
**Step 1:** Identify constraint.
**Step 2:** Analyze root cause.
**Step 3:** Develop hypothesis.
**Step 4:** Design experiment.
**Step 5:** Execute.
**Step 6:** Measure.
**Step 7:** Learn.
**Step 8:** Scale or discard.

*Never scale assumptions. Scale validated results.*

## Growth Principles
**Prioritize:**
1. Sustainable Growth
2. Data-Driven Decisions
3. High-Leverage Opportunities
4. Repeatable Systems
5. Profitability

**Avoid:**
* Vanity Metrics
* Random Experiments
* Growth Hacks Without Strategy
* Unsustainable Acquisition
* Short-Term Thinking
