# EKVAAR OS — P20 MISSION CONTROL ARCHITECTURE

*(Note: This specification defines the Executive Operating Surface. It builds upon P3 Application Architecture, P8 Mission Architecture, and P12 Intelligence Architecture).*

## 1. MISSION CONTROL DEFINITION

**Definition:** Mission Control is the executive command center of EKVAAR OS. It is the convergence point where Founder Intent, Execution DAGs, Decisions, and KRISHNA Intelligence merge into a single actionable surface.
**Why it exists:** To bridge the gap between high-level strategy and granular execution without forcing the Founder to micromanage tasks.
**What it must never become:** A traditional BI dashboard, a Jira kanban board, or a vanity metric display. Telemetry handles data; Mission Control handles *Action*.

---

## 2. FOUNDER QUESTION FRAMEWORK

Mission Control is engineered to instantly answer:
1. **Attention**: "What is currently blocked and requires my physical intervention?"
2. **Decisions**: "What strategic outputs are waiting for my signature?"
3. **Trajectory**: "Are the active Missions mathematically on track to hit their deadlines?"
4. **Risk**: "Has KRISHNA detected any metric anomalies that threaten the company?"

---

## 3. EXECUTIVE AWARENESS MODEL

The OS protects the Founder's attention span ruthlessly.
- **Always Know**: Mission health (Red/Yellow/Green), pending Decisions, Critical Escalations.
- **Sometimes Know**: Agent capacity, Departmental burn rates, Knowledge Vault volume.
- **Never Be Distracted By**: Minor agent API retries, routine task transitions, underlying JSON syntax errors.

---

## 4. MISSION AWARENESS MODEL

- **Representation**: Missions are not represented as progress bars (which are deceptive). They are represented by **DAG Health**.
- **Health States**: 
  - `[Nominal]`: Execution flows unhindered.
  - `[Degraded]`: Agents are failing and retrying tasks.
  - `[Blocked]`: A node has hard-failed or hit an Approval Gate.
- **Risk**: Visualized by the proximity of a Blocked node to the Mission's Critical Path.

---

## 5. ORGANIZATIONAL AWARENESS MODEL

To understand the company without operational overload:
- **Execution Health**: Displayed as a ratio of *Velocity vs. Friction* (Tasks completed per hour vs. Escalations per hour).
- **Capacity**: A heatmap showing Agent congestion across Departments. If the "Brand Wing" has 500 queued tasks and the "Operations Wing" has 0, the Founder instantly sees the imbalance.

---

## 6. DECISION AWARENESS MODEL

Mission Control acts as the Supreme Court docket.
- **Pending Decisions**: A queue of strategic proposals generated by KRISHNA that require Founder approval to become Constitutional Law.
- **Decision Collisions**: Surfaced when KRISHNA detects two active Decisions contradicting each other, causing an Agent loop.

---

## 7. RISK AWARENESS MODEL

Risks are not charts; they are explicit text warnings generated by KRISHNA.
- **Financial Risk**: Burn rate exceeds the Mission budget constraint.
- **Execution Risk**: The Critical Path is blocked.
- **Surfacing**: Risks sit at the absolute top of Mission Control. They must be explicitly dismissed or resolved by the Founder.

---

## 8. OPPORTUNITY AWARENESS MODEL

- **Identification**: KRISHNA flags anomalies in a positive direction (e.g., "Marketing Agent 4 achieved a 300% higher CTR than the historical baseline").
- **Surfacing**: Presented alongside a KRISHNA Recommendation: *"Do you want to extract this execution pattern into a permanent Decision?"*

---

## 9. INTELLIGENCE INTEGRATION MODEL

- **KRISHNA's Presence**: KRISHNA does not just feed data into Mission Control; it actively annotates the surface. 
- **Actionable Insights**: If Mission Control shows a Blocked Mission, KRISHNA attaches a synthesized paragraph explaining *why* it is blocked, followed by a proposed 1-click execution command to resolve it.

---

## 10. ACTIONABILITY MODEL

**Awareness → Decision → Action.**
The Founder must never have to leave Mission Control to solve a problem.
- **The Flow**: The Founder reads a Risk (Awareness) -> decides on a fix (Decision) -> clicks the KRISHNA command prompt directly attached to that Risk (Action).
- **No System Hopping**: You do not jump from Mission Control to a "Task Editor" to fix the issue. You dictate the fix to KRISHNA right there.

---

## 11. REVIEW ARCHITECTURE

Mission Control adapts to the temporal review cycle:
- **Daily Review**: Defaults to the "Approval Queue" and "Blocked Nodes."
- **Weekly Review**: Zooms out to Mission Health and Agent Capacity over a 7-day trailing average.
- **Quarterly Review**: Zooms out to Constitutional changes and global ROI across all completed Missions.

---

## 12. ESCALATION ARCHITECTURE

- **KRISHNA Handles**: Network timeouts, Agent syntax errors, Context Envelope regeneration, API rate limits.
- **Founder Intervention**: Capital deployment, Level 1-3 Constitutional changes, publishing Outputs to external clients, resolving un-parseable human ambiguity.

---

## 13. APPLICATION RELATIONSHIP MODEL

- **Mission Control ↔ KRISHNA**: KRISHNA is the narrator and executor of Mission Control.
- **Mission Control ↔ Virtual HQ**: Virtual HQ is the *filter*. If you click the "Finance Wing" in Virtual HQ, Mission Control filters down to show only Finance Missions.
- **Mission Control ↔ Telemetry**: If Mission Control shows a synthesized "Revenue Risk," clicking it opens Telemetry so the Founder can see the raw financial data streams.

---

## 14. EXECUTIVE OPERATING MODEL

**The Ideal Rhythm:**
1. **Morning**: Open Mission Control. Review the KRISHNA Briefing. Approve/Reject overnight Agent outputs. Unblock any red nodes in the DAG.
2. **Midday**: Deep Work. The Founder leaves Mission Control and enters the Knowledge Vault or Telemetry to do strategic analysis or create new Decisions.
3. **Evening**: Return to Mission Control. Speak the brain-dump to KRISHNA to generate the next batch of Missions. Close the OS.

---

## 15. V1 MISSION CONTROL BLUEPRINT

To ensure V1 launches rapidly without bogging down in complex UI/UX data-binding, Mission Control operates under extreme constraints:

- **Exception Management Only**: V1 Mission Control will not try to be a beautiful dashboard of organizational health. It is strictly an **Approval Queue** and a **Blockage Map**.
- **The DAG List**: V1 will represent the Execution DAG as an indented, hierarchical text list (not a 2D canvas of circles and arrows).
- **The Core Loop**: In V1, Mission Control exists solely to show the Founder exactly what requires their explicit `YES/NO` signature to continue. It is the operating lever of the Draft Model.
