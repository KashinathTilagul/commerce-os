Why Commerce OS
Why Commerce OS

One desktop.
Your whole business.

Commerce OS replaces 12 different SaaS tools with one macOS-style workspace — so your team and AI agents operate in one shared context.

OS-Level Context
Every module shares a single data layer. No silos, no copy-paste, no lag between inventory and orders.
AI Workforce Built-In
CEO, CMO, CTO, CFO, and COO agents run autonomously and escalate only when governance requires.
Omnichannel Orders
Shopify, WooCommerce, Razorpay, Amazon and 800+ channels — all orders in one unified queue.
Smart Inventory
Live stock, AI-generated reorder triggers, and supplier POs raised before you run out — automatically.
Finance at a Glance
Live P&L, cash flow, and margin tracking. CFO agent files summaries and flags issues before month-end.
Campaign Intelligence
CMO agent monitors ROAS across Meta, Google, and WhatsApp — pauses underperformers, scales winners.
