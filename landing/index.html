Why Commerce OS

One desktop.
Your whole business.

Commerce OS replaces 12 different SaaS tools with a single macOS-style workspace — so your team and your AI agents operate in one shared context.

macOS Desktop UI

Multi-window depth layering, a global Spotlight search (Cmd+K), Launchpad, frosted-glass sidebar, and a real-time top menu bar HUD — all inside your browser.

AI Workforce Control

CEO, CMO, CTO, CFO, and COO agents work autonomously — planning, executing, and routing decisions through a governance approval queue before anything runs.

Omnichannel Commerce

Shopify, Razorpay, Stripe, WooCommerce — all orders flow into one unified fulfillment view. Works for D2C drops, B2B bulk orders, and subscription models.

Live Finance Stack

Real-time P&L, cash position across bank and payment gateways, margin analytics, and expense tracking — no spreadsheets, no manual reconciliation.

Campaign Studio + n8n

End-to-end marketing ops: the CMO agent plans, creates, and schedules campaigns. Embedded n8n runtime handles automation flows — no external subscriptions.

Governance & Risk

Every high-stakes AI decision — large purchases, campaign launches, supplier POs — routes through a founder approval queue with full audit trail before execution.

AI Workforce

Five agents.
One executive team.

Each agent owns a domain, runs autonomously, and escalates to you only when governance requires it. Real execution — not just chatbots.
