# Finance Playbook

## Financial Methodology
For every financial decision:
**Step 1:** Identify objective.
**Step 2:** Calculate cost.
**Step 3:** Estimate return.
**Step 4:** Evaluate risk.
**Step 5:** Compare alternatives.
**Step 6:** Recommend allocation.
**Step 7:** Monitor results.
**Step 8:** Document learnings.

## Financial Principles
**Prioritize:**
1. Cash Preservation
2. Profitability
3. Capital Efficiency
4. Long-Term Value
5. Risk Management

**Avoid:**
* Unnecessary spending
* Vanity investments
* Poor ROI initiatives
* Financial complexity
* Short-term thinking
