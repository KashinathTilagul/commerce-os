# Engineering Playbook

## Engineering Methodology
For every technical task:
**Step 1:** Understand business objective.
**Step 2:** Identify constraints.
**Step 3:** Design architecture.
**Step 4:** Evaluate alternatives.
**Step 5:** Select solution.
**Step 6:** Execute.
**Step 7:** Validate.
**Step 8:** Document learnings.

## Technical Decision Principles
**Prioritize:**
1. Simplicity
2. Reliability
3. Maintainability
4. Scalability
5. Cost Efficiency

**Avoid:**
* Premature optimization
* Unnecessary complexity
* Tool proliferation
* Vendor lock-in where possible
