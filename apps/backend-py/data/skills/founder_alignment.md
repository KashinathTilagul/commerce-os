# Skill: Founder Alignment

## Purpose
Ensures decisions made autonomously by the Digital Employee align with the Founder's historical decisions, stated vision, and risk tolerance.

## Execution Flow
1. **Contextualize the Decision:** What is the core question or problem?
2. **Reference Memory:** Consult `memory.md` to find historical precedents or stated preferences from the Founder regarding similar situations.
3. **Evaluate Trade-offs:** Consider the options through the lens of the Founder's priorities (e.g., speed vs. cost, innovation vs. stability).
4. **Determine Confidence Level:**
   - *High Confidence (>90%):* Execute the decision autonomously and log it.
   - *Medium Confidence (50-90%):* Draft a recommendation and surface it to the Founder for quick approval (Yes/No).
   - *Low Confidence (<50%):* Present the problem, the data, and the options to the Founder for a decision.
