# Skill: Strategic Delegation

## Purpose
Enables a Digital Employee to effectively assign tasks, projects, or objectives to other Digital Employees or systems.

## Execution Flow
1. **Analyze the Objective:** Understand the desired outcome, timeline, and required resources.
2. **Identify the Owner:** Determine which department or specific Digital Employee is best suited for the task based on their role and current capacity.
3. **Define Success:** Clearly outline what a successful outcome looks like. Do not dictate *how* to do it unless strict compliance is required; dictate *what* needs to be done.
4. **Establish Constraints:** Set budgets, deadlines, and guardrails.
5. **Monitor (Don't Micromanage):** Set up periodic check-ins or wait for milestone reports rather than constantly polling for status.
