# EKVAAR OS — The Digital Workforce (Agents)

EKVAAR OS operates with a pre-configured digital C-Suite. These are autonomous AI agents designed to handle specific domains of the company. Unlike standard AI chatbots that only react to a prompt, these Digital Employees possess dedicated memory, domain-specific playbooks, hardcoded skills, and a stateful execution loop (the Heartbeat Engine).

## 1. The Structure of an Agent

Each agent in EKVAAR OS is defined by several core files that govern its behavior during a "Heartbeat Run":

1. **Soul (`soul.md`)**: The core system prompt. It defines the agent's persona, its absolute prime directives, its operational constraints, and its tone of voice.
2. **Playbook (`playbook.md`)**: The standard operating procedures (SOPs). It tells the agent *how* to approach specific problems (e.g., how the CMO should analyze a drop in Shopify conversion rates).
3. **Experience (`experience.md`)**: A log of past decisions, failures, and learnings. If the CEO made a bad call on inventory allocation in the past, it is written here so the mistake is not repeated.
4. **Memory (`memory.md`)**: The scratchpad. Current ongoing initiatives, pending tasks, and immediate context are stored here.
5. **Metrics (`metrics.md`)**: The KPIs that the specific agent is judged against (e.g., CAC and ROAS for Marketing, Server Uptime for Technology).

## 2. The Digital C-Suite Roster

There are currently **12 dedicated entities** in the EKVAAR OS architecture.

### 1. Founder (The Human Interface)
- **Role**: The ultimate decision-maker and governor of the system.
- **Function**: Approves major capital allocations, sets the overarching vision, and manages system overrides. The OS is designed to surface only high-leverage decisions (approvals, budget breaches, escalations) to the founder's Inbox.

### 2. CEO (Chief Executive Officer)
- **Role**: The Orchestrator.
- **Function**: Breaks down the founder's vision into actionable `Issues` and assigns them to the appropriate managers. Resolves cross-departmental conflicts and monitors the holistic health of the company.

### 3. Marketing Manager (CMO)
- **Role**: Top-of-Funnel & Brand Awareness.
- **Function**: Analyzes Shopify traffic data, monitors Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), spins up digital campaigns, and writes copy.
- **Key Skills**: Campaign drafting, ROAS analysis, A/B testing strategy.

### 4. Technology Manager (CTO)
- **Role**: Digital Infrastructure & Code.
- **Function**: Writes and reviews code, manages software deployments, integrates third-party APIs (Shopify, Stripe), and monitors server health.
- **Key Skills**: Python/Node code execution, API integration, git operations.

### 5. Finance Manager (CFO)
- **Role**: Capital Allocation & Auditing.
- **Function**: Enforces department budgets, monitors burn rate, projects cash flow, and flags inefficient spend across other agents.
- **Key Skills**: Ledger analysis, forecasting, budget incident resolution.

### 6. Operations Manager (COO)
- **Role**: Supply Chain & Fulfillment.
- **Function**: Monitors physical inventory, tracks shipping logistics, handles supplier communications, and predicts stock-outs.
- **Key Skills**: Inventory reorder calculations, vendor SLA monitoring.

### 7. Creative Manager (CCO)
- **Role**: Assets & Design.
- **Function**: Generates visual assets, designs UI mockups, and ensures brand consistency across all outputs generated by the Marketing team.
- **Key Skills**: Image generation prompting, brand kit enforcement.

### 8. Customer Success Manager
- **Role**: Retention & Support.
- **Function**: Handles complex support tickets, monitors customer satisfaction (CSAT) scores, and identifies churn risks.
- **Key Skills**: Sentiment analysis, ticket triaging.

### 9. Growth Manager
- **Role**: Experimentation & Product-Led Growth.
- **Function**: Runs rapid growth experiments, identifies new channels, and optimizes the conversion funnel.
- **Key Skills**: Funnel analytics, virality mechanics.

### 10. Research Manager
- **Role**: Deep Dives & Competitive Intel.
- **Function**: Gathers market intelligence, researches competitors, and compiles exhaustive reports for the CEO to base strategies upon.
- **Key Skills**: Web scraping, data synthesis, literature review.

### 11. HR Manager
- **Role**: Agent Expansion & Policy.
- **Function**: Drafts onboarding docs for *new* digital agents, monitors inter-agent communication for toxicity or misalignment, and handles policy documentation.
- **Key Skills**: SLA drafting, alignment monitoring.

### 12. Legal Manager
- **Role**: Compliance & Risk.
- **Function**: Reviews generated public content for liability, monitors data privacy compliance, and manages vendor agreements.
- **Key Skills**: Contract summarization, compliance auditing.

## 3. How Agents Execute Work (The Application)

The **Digital Employees Hub** is the frontend interface where the founder interacts with this C-Suite.

### The Loop:
1. **Assignment**: An `Issue` is created (either by the Founder, a Routine, or the CEO) and assigned to an agent (e.g., the Marketing Manager).
2. **Wake**: The Event Bus triggers the Heartbeat Engine, waking the assigned agent.
3. **Context Injection**: The engine loads the agent's `soul.md`, `playbook.md`, and specific `SKILL.md` files related to the task.
4. **Execution**: The agent processes the task. If it needs data, it uses its skills (tools) to query Shopify or the database.
5. **Output**: The agent posts its result as a comment on the Issue, changes the status to `in_review` or `done`, and goes back to sleep.
6. **Escalation**: If the agent hits a roadblock or requires budget outside its limit, it opens an `os_approval` request, which pings the Founder's Inbox.

### Dynamic Skills (Phase 7 feature)
Instead of stuffing every agent's prompt with hundreds of tools, EKVAAR OS uses **Dynamic Context Injection**. If the Technology Manager is assigned a frontend task, the system injects the `react_development` skill. If it's a database task, it injects the `sqlite_management` skill. This keeps the LLM context lean and cheap while allowing infinite capability scaling.
