# Research Dashboard Architecture

## Key Views
1. **Live Intelligence Feed:** Real-time stream of market, competitor, and trend updates.
2. **Opportunity Matrix:** Ranked grid of discovered opportunities based on Impact, Effort, and Risk.
3. **Risk Radar:** Visual map of impending market, competitive, and operational risks.
4. **Competitor Watchlist:** Dashboard tracking competitor pricing, products, and campaigns.
5. **Capacity Monitor:** Real-time visualization of current load and queue depth.
