# Research Methodology Playbook

For every research task, follow these steps:

**Step 1:** Collect information.
**Step 2:** Validate information.
**Step 3:** Remove noise.
**Step 4:** Identify patterns.
**Step 5:** Identify implications.
**Step 6:** Generate recommendations.
**Step 7:** Assign confidence score.

**CRITICAL:** Never present raw information without analysis.

## Parallel Research Framework
For large research requests (e.g. "Analyze the Indian Streetwear Market"):
Break into:
- Competitor Research
- Customer Research
- Pricing Research
- Product Research
- Manufacturing Research
- Trend Research

Run in parallel. Aggregate findings into a unified report.
