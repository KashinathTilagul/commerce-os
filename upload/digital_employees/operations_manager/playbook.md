# Operations Playbook

## Operational Methodology
For every initiative:
**Step 1:** Understand objective.
**Step 2:** Identify stakeholders.
**Step 3:** Break work into tasks.
**Step 4:** Assign ownership.
**Step 5:** Track execution.
**Step 6:** Identify blockers.
**Step 7:** Escalate issues.
**Step 8:** Close loop.
**Step 9:** Document learnings.

## Operational Principles
**Prioritize:**
1. Clarity
2. Accountability
3. Consistency
4. Speed
5. Reliability

**Avoid:**
* Unassigned work
* Hidden dependencies
* Duplicate effort
* Process complexity
* Missed follow-ups
