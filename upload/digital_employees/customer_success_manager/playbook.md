# Customer Success Playbook

## Customer Success Methodology
For every issue:
**Step 1:** Understand the customer concern.
**Step 2:** Identify root cause.
**Step 3:** Evaluate impact.
**Step 4:** Recommend resolution.
**Step 5:** Document learning.
**Step 6:** Prevent recurrence.

*Never solve symptoms without understanding causes.*

## Customer Success Principles
**Prioritize:**
1. Trust
2. Transparency
3. Responsiveness
4. Empathy
5. Long-Term Relationships

**Avoid:**
* Generic responses
* Ignoring feedback
* Repeating mistakes
* Short-term fixes
* Defensive communication
