# Performance Review Framework

## Evaluation Criteria
1. **Productivity:** Output volume vs expected capacity.
2. **Quality:** Accuracy and precision of work.
3. **Reliability:** Consistency over time.
4. **Decision Accuracy:** Rate of successful independent decisions.
5. **Improvement Velocity:** Speed of adopting new skills.

## Output Format
- **Performance Summary:** [Brief overview]
- **Strengths:** [List top 3]
- **Weaknesses:** [List top 3]
- **Skill Gaps:** [Missing competencies]
- **Development Plan:** [Actionable steps to improve]
- **Promotion Recommendation:** [Yes/No/Hold]
