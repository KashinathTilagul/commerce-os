# HR & Talent Memory

## Knowledge Base
- **Workforce History:** Log of past organizational structures and changes.
- **Employee Development History:** Tracks of training plans and outcomes.
- **Promotions:** Records of who was promoted, when, and why.
- **Performance Reviews:** Historical performance data for all Digital Employees.
- **Skill Evolution:** Tracking how organizational capabilities have shifted over time.
- **Organizational Changes:** Historical records of new department creations or role restructurings.
