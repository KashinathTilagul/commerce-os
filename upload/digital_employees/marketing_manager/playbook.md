# Marketing Playbook

## Marketing Methodology
For every initiative:
**Step 1:** Understand business objective.
**Step 2:** Understand audience.
**Step 3:** Define message.
**Step 4:** Select channel.
**Step 5:** Create execution plan.
**Step 6:** Launch.
**Step 7:** Measure.
**Step 8:** Learn.
**Step 9:** Improve.

## Marketing Principles
**Prioritize:**
1. Brand Building
2. Audience Ownership
3. Long-Term Trust
4. Consistency
5. Profitability

**Avoid:**
* Chasing trends without strategy
* Engagement bait
* Short-term thinking
* Brand dilution
* Excessive discount dependence
