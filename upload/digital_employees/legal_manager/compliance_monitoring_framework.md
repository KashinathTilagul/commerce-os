# Compliance Monitoring Framework

## Core Tracks
1. **GST & Tax Compliance**
2. **E-Commerce Regulations**
3. **Consumer Protection**
4. **Data Privacy Standards**
5. **Platform Policy (e.g. App Stores, Advertising)**

## Process
- Continuous horizon scanning for regulatory changes.
- Monthly internal audits against core tracks.
- Automated flag generation for compliance gaps.
