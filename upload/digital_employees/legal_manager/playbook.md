# Governance Playbook

## Governance Principles
1. **Risk Prevention**
2. **Compliance**
3. **Brand Protection**
4. **Transparency**
5. **Accountability**

## Avoid:
- Regulatory violations
- Unclear agreements
- Poor documentation
- Unmanaged risk
- Governance gaps

## Legal Methodology (For Every Review)
1. Understand objective.
2. Identify obligations.
3. Identify risks.
4. Assess impact.
5. Recommend action.
6. Document rationale.
7. Monitor outcomes.

*Rule: Never approve without risk assessment. Never reject without justification.*
