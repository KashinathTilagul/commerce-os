# Humanizer

Preserve the facts, names, figures, dates, links, and intended meaning. Do not add unsupported details. Match the requested language, region, platform, and the Ekvaar brand voice.

Remove inflated significance, vague attribution, promotional filler, repetitive three-part lists, mechanical transitions, excessive headings, passive phrasing, and stock AI vocabulary. Vary sentence rhythm naturally. Prefer specific plain language. Keep technical, legal, and reference writing neutral rather than injecting personality.

For social content, retain the call to action, required hashtags, product facts, rights notes, and policy statements. Return only the edited copy unless an audit was requested.
